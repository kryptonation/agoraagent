## 📝 LinkedIn Post Draft

**Post Body:**

Procurement is broken. 

We spend weeks negotiating software contracts, consulting vendor pools, and analyzing bids. Yet, studies show that mid-market businesses still leave up to 15%–20% of contract value on the table due to poor pricing benchmarks and deal fatigue.

What if we could let autonomous AI agents do the heavy lifting—guided by the mathematics of Game Theory?

Enter **AgoraAgent** 🤖📊

We’ve just built a working prototype of a game-theoretic negotiation and simulation engine. Instead of static RFPs or painful email loops, AgoraAgent runs multi-agent simulations (powered by LangGraph and AWS Bedrock) to find the absolute boundary of what’s possible before you sign a contract.

Here are the key USPs we’ve built in:
1. **Game-Theoretic "Shadow Play" Simulator:** Runs up to 15 parallel mock negotiations in seconds. It maps the counterparty's behavior and aggregates statistics to advise you on when to walk away or hold firm.
2. **Coordinated Bundle Sourcing:** Negotiates sequential services (e.g., Design ➡️ Dev ➡️ Database) under a single budget pool, dynamically reallocating negotiated savings from early vendors to expand the budget of subsequent ones.
3. **Shapley-Value Coalition Sourcing:** Allows smaller buyers to group together, buy in bulk, and distributes the volume-discount savings mathematically and transparently using cooperative game theory formulas (Shapley Value payouts).
4. **Semantic Archetype Classifiers:** Reads counterparty replies in real-time, predicts their style (Aggressive, Collaborative, Conceding, Avoidant), and injects prompt-level strategic counter-strategies on the fly.

We are thinking about turning this into a proper SaaS platform. But before we do, we want to hear from the community.

- Which of the 3 sourcing engines (Shadow Play, Bundle, or Coalition) would drive the most value for your team?
- What are the compliance or trust boundaries you would need?

## 💡 Best Practices for Publishing on LinkedIn
1. **Add Link in Comments:** If you want to link to your live demo ([https://at24emesxq.ap-south-1.awsapprunner.com](https://at24emesxq.ap-south-1.awsapprunner.com)) or a GitHub repository, **do not put it in the post body**. The LinkedIn algorithm penalizes posts with external outbound links. Instead, write *"Demo link in the comments below!"* and post it as the first comment after publishing.
2. **Format the Spacing:** Make sure the short paragraph spacing is maintained. High scannability increases the read-through rate by 3x.
3. **Engage in the First 2 Hours:** Respond to every comment you get within the first 1–2 hours. This signals the algorithm to push your post to secondary connections.
